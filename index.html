A performance marketer uses data-driven strategies to run and optimize digital campaigns with measurable goals such as leads, sales, bookings, and conversions. The focus is on improving results and maximizing return on ad spend (ROAS).
I work with platforms such as Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), YouTube Ads, and other relevant advertising channels depending on your business and target audience.
Results depend on your industry, budget, offer, audience, and campaign goals. Some campaigns can generate results within days, while others need several weeks of testing and optimization to reach their potential.
Pricing depends on the scope of work, advertising budget, number of platforms, and campaign complexity. I offer customized packages based on your specific business goals rather than a one-size-fits-all price.
No ethical marketer can guarantee a specific number of sales or leads. What I can guarantee is a structured, data-driven approach with continuous testing, optimization, and transparent reporting focused on improving campaign performance.
I’ll typically need information about your business, target audience, products or services, goals, previous campaign data, website or landing page access, and advertising account access where required.
