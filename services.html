FAQ

Got
questions?

01What does a performance marketer do?+
I focus on driving measurable results—like leads, sales, and bookings—using data-driven strategies across paid ads, SEO, and conversion optimization.
02How much do your services cost?+
Pricing depends on the scope of the project. I offer custom packages tailored to your specific business goals and budget requirements.
03How soon can I see results?+
While SEO takes 3-6 months to mature, paid ad campaigns often start generating qualified traffic and leads within the first two weeks.
04Which platforms do you work with?+
I specialize in Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram), LinkedIn Ads, WordPress, and Next.js / modern web tech.
05Can you guarantee leads or sales?+
No professional can guarantee specific sales numbers due to market variables, but I do guarantee a data-driven process designed to maximize your ROI.
06What do you need from me to get started?+
I usually need access to your current analytics/ad accounts, brand assets, and a 30-minute discovery call to align on your goals.
07Do you create ad creatives as well?+
Yes, I offer end-to-end content production including ad graphics, video editing, and copywriting.
08Do you work with small businesses?+
Absolutely. I love helping small and local businesses scale into larger, more profitable organizations.
09Can you help with website optimization?+
Yes! Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) is a huge part of my process. A great ad is useless if the website doesn't convert.
10Why should I hire you?+
Because I operate without agency bloat. You get direct communication, transparent reporting, and strategies focused strictly on your bottom line.
